What is the hotel's location?+

We are located at Calle 10A #36-29 in the El Poblado neighborhood of Medellín (Colombia).

It is exactly the southeastern area of Medellín, and is formally known as Comuna 14. It limits to the north with the city center and to the south with the municipality of Envigado.

It is one of the city’s most prominent and exclusive areas, renowned for its hotels, shopping centers, restaurants and lively nightlife.

What major tourist attractions or sites are near the hotel?+

El Poblado, one of Medellín’s most iconic neighborhoods, offers a variety of attractions that combine culture, gastronomy, and nightlife. Here are five must-see places for international visitors to make the most of their stay in this vibrant area:

Lleras Park.

Considered the epicenter of Medellín’s nightlife, it’s surrounded by a wide variety of bars, restaurants, and nightclubs. It’s the ideal place to experience the city’s vibrant nightlife and socialize with locals and tourists.

The Castle Museum and Gardens.

Inspired by the castles of the Loire Valley in France, this museum offers a showcase of European art and architecture right in the heart of Medellín. Its gardens are ideal for leisurely strolls and picnics, providing a peaceful haven away from the urban bustle.

Museum of Modern Art of Medellín (MAMM).

Located in the Ciudad del Río area, the MAMM presents contemporary art exhibitions and is a cultural hub. Its modern architecture and frequent events make it a must-see for art lovers.

Poblado Park.

This park is a historical landmark, marking the site of the first settlement in the Aburrá Valley. Surrounded by cafes and shops, it’s an ideal place to relax and observe daily life in the city.

Santafé Shopping Center.

This shopping center is one of the largest in Medellín and offers a wide variety of shops, restaurants, and entertainment. Its modern design and themed events make it a popular spot for both locals and tourists..

Is the area where the hotel is located safe, especially for walking at night?+

El Poblado is considered one of the safest and most popular tourist areas in Medellín, attracting many visitors with its vibrant nightlife, restaurants, and shops. However, it’s important for travelers to take certain precautions to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience. Here are some practical tips for those planning a visit to El Poblado:

- Recommended areas: Places like Provenza, Parque Lleras, and Parque El Poblado are popular and usually well-monitored. These areas have a constant presence of tourists and residents, which contributes to a safer environment.

- Avoid unfamiliar areas: It is advisable not to venture into unfamiliar streets or neighborhoods, especially at night, as they may lack adequate lighting and police presence.

- Safe transport: After dark, it’s best to use reliable transportation services, such as licensed taxis or ride-hailing apps. This is especially important when traveling between areas or returning to your accommodation.

- Avoid walking alone: While El Poblado is generally safe, it’s advisable to avoid walking alone at night on deserted or poorly lit streets. Opt for walking in groups and sticking to well-lit routes.

- Be discreet with valuables: Avoid displaying jewelry, expensive electronics, or other valuables in public to avoid attracting attention.

- Constant surveillance: Keep your belongings, such as bags and backpacks, in sight and secure at all times, especially in crowded places like bars and restaurants.

- Transportation apps: Use approved apps to request transportation and always verify driver and vehicle information before getting in.

- Secure connections: When using public Wi-Fi networks, avoid accessing sensitive information and consider using a VPN to protect your data.

- Proceed with caution in new relationships: While local hospitality is extraordinary, maintain a cautious attitude when interacting with people you have just met, especially at night.

- Inform others: Let your friends or family know your plans and location, especially if you decide to explore the nightlife or participate in activities with new acquaintances.

- Stay informed: Consult local and reliable sources to stay informed of any security alerts or warnings in the area.

- Emergency assistance: Keep the contact numbers for local authorities and your consulate or embassy handy in case you need help.

In summary, while El Poblado is one of the safest and most attractive areas in Medellín, it is important for travelers to remain vigilant and follow these tips to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience during their visit.
