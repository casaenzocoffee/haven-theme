Known as the "City of Eternal Spring"

Named for its mild and pleasant climate throughout the year.

Medellín is a fascinating destination that combines culture, history, innovation, and a vibrant nightlife. If you're planning your first visit from the United States, here's a detailed guide with the 10 best things to do and see.

Get ready to explore this magical city and its surroundings.

1. Explore the Historic Center and Botero Sculpture Park. Why go?

Downtown Medellín is the city's historical and cultural heart. Here you'll find a blend of colonial and modern architecture, as well as many of the famous sculptures by Colombian artist Fernando Botero.

Things to do

  • Botero Sculpture Park: Admire 23 of Botero's most iconic sculptures. It's an ideal place to take photos and learn more about the master's unique style.
  • Museum of Antioquia: Located next to the park, this museum houses one of the largest collections and exhibitions of Botero's work, which tell the story of Medellín and Colombia.
  • Metropolitan Basilica: An architectural gem built entirely of brick, perfect for lovers of history and design.

Advice

Take a guided tour to better understand the history and contrasts of the city.

2. Visit to Comuna 13: a symbol of transformation. Why go?

Comuna 13 is a story of resilience and social transformation. Once one of the most dangerous areas, it is now an example of how art and community can transform lives.

To do?

  • Graffiti Tour: Explore the vibrant streets filled with murals that tell stories of hope and resilience.
  • Escalators: A unique system that connects the community to the city. A symbol of social innovation.
  • Hip Hop and Urban Dance Shows: Enjoy local talent live.

Advice

Visit the area with a local guide for a safe and authentic experience.

3. Climb Cerro Nutibara and visit Pueblito Paisa. Why go?

Nutibara Hill offers one of the best panoramic views of Medellín. At the top, Pueblito Paisa recreates a typical Antioquian village.

To do?

  • Lookout: Enjoy a 360-degree panoramic view of the city.
  • Handicrafts and souvenirs: Buy souvenirs made by local artisans.
  • Gastronomy: Try typical dishes like bandeja paisa or arepas de chócolo.

4. Discover the nightlife of the Provenza district. Why go?

Provenza, in El Poblado, is the epicenter of Medellín's nightlife. Here you'll find top-notch restaurants, bars, and nightclubs.

To do?

  • For lunch: Dine at places like El Cielo (signature cuisine) or Carmen (Colombian and international fusion).
  • Bars: DEnjoy cocktails at Alambique or craft beers at 3 Cordilleras.
  • Nightclubs: Dance all night at places like La Octava or Vintrash.

Advice

Start early to enjoy dinner and then go to the nightclubs.

5. Visit to Guatapé and El Peñol: An excursion to nearby towns. Why go?

Guatapé is a picturesque town located two hours from Medellín, known for its decorative baseboards and the imposing Piedra del Peñol.

What to do in Guatapé?

  • Climb to the Rock of the Rock: Climb the 740 steps to enjoy spectacular views of the reservoir.
  • Explore the city of Guatapé: Stroll through its colorful streets and enjoy the local culture.
  • Boat trip: Sail across the reservoir and explore the islands.

Advice

Book a tour or rent a car to make the most of your day.

6. Visit the Botanical Garden and Explora Park. Why go?

This area is ideal for families and lovers of nature and science.

To do?

  • Botanical garden: Relax among the native flora and enjoy the temporary exhibitions.
  • Explora Park: An interactive science and technology museum with one of the largest aquariums in Latin America.

Advice

Set aside half a day to explore both places at your own pace.

7. Take the Metrocable and discover Arví Park. Why go?

The Metrocable is not just a means of transport, but also an experience that offers stunning views and connects the city with the countryside.

To do?

  • Take the Metrocable: Take line L to Arví Park.
  • Arví Park: Enjoy eco-walks, picnics, or guided forest tours.

8. Experience tango in Medellín. Why go?

Medellín has a strong connection with tango, thanks to Carlos Gardel, who died in the city.

To do?

  • Gardeliana House Museum: Learn about the history of tango in Medellín.
  • Milongas and tango shows: Enjoy live shows at venues like El Patio del Tango.

9. Prueba la comida local ¿Por qué ir?

Paisa cuisine is a must-try.

To do?

  • Bandeja Paisa: Try this traditional dish that includes ground beef, chorizo, plantains, rice, beans, and more.
  • Desserts: Try the custard and fritters, especially during the Christmas season.
  • Local Markets: Visit the Retail Market or the Flower Square Market to discover authentic flavors.

10. Visit the flower fair (if it's in season). Why go?

It is the most important event in Medellín, celebrated every August, and a celebration of the Paisa culture.

To do?

  • Silletero Parade: Observe the farmers displaying flowers in colorful arrangements.
  • Cultural events: Attend concerts, exhibitions, and craft fairs.

Practical tips for your visit

  • Transport: Use the subway and apps like Uber to get around easily.
  • Security: Although Medellín is much safer today, be careful in crowded places.
  • Currency: Bring Colombian pesos, as not all places accept dollars or credit cards.

Medellín will surprise you with its hospitality, its rich culture, and its vibrant energy.

Pack your bags for an unforgettable experience!
