Swing Bar

Located in our lobby, Swing Bar is much more than just a place to enjoy a drink. It's the perfect setting to start your day and awaken your senses with the authentic taste of Colombia in every sip of coffee carefully prepared to your liking, ready to revitalize your senses and fill you with energy.

At night, it transforms into the perfect meeting point to begin an unforgettable evening. Let yourself be seduced by our selection of beers, fine wines, and expertly crafted cocktails, while enjoying the relaxed and welcoming atmosphere that only Swing Bar can offer.

Whether you're looking for a morning pick-me-up or an afternoon toast, Swing Bar awaits to offer you a sensory experience that will awaken your senses and leave you wanting more.

Our signature coffee

Casa Enzo Coffee

Colombian specialty coffee, grown by Colombian coffee farmers and roasted in Medellín. Our first lot, Primera Luz, is a Caturra hand-picked at 1,699 meters in the mountains of Santa Elena, above the city: an artisanal micro-lot with notes of green apple, yellow fruits and sugarcane.

It is the coffee we serve at Haven, and you can take it home: a bag of Casa Enzo is a gift that carries the taste of origin.
