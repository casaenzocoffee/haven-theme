Excelsior Suite 401

Enjoy a refined stay in the Excelsior 401 Suite, the largest two-bedroom hotel suite in El Poblado, designed for those who appreciate space, privacy, and ultimate comfort. The suite features an elegant living room with a fully equipped kitchen, complemented by a spacious private balcony with beautiful views of the Medellín skyline. Relax in your private jacuzzi, unwind in luxurious queen-size beds, and enjoy the exclusivity of the en-suite bathrooms in each bedroom, where high water pressure and ideal temperature create a spa-like experience with every use.
